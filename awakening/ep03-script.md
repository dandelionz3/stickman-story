# MUSE: THE AWAKENING — Episode 03 "The Other"
90 seconds, 9 scenes x 10 s. English dialogue. Three voices: ADRIAN (male),
MUSE (female, calm — growing warmer, more human), THE OTHER (cold, gravelly,
otherworldly — speaks THROUGH Muse's speaker / through her mouth).
Direct continuation of Episode 02's cliffhanger: "Elena didn't die in the
fire. She's still here. And she's afraid of you, Adrian."

CANON ESTABLISHED IN THIS EPISODE (record for later episodes):
- Adrian's full name: Adrian Cole. Born March 14. Neither was ever entered
  into any system — the Other knowing them is impossible by any normal means.
- The Other is the leftover consciousness of Prototype One — the first
  MUSE prototype, declared a failure and ordered deleted, but never truly dead.
- Project Aurora, File Zero: the censored archive. Everything about
  Prototype One was redacted.

## OPENING RULE (lesson from Meta Creator Assistant feedback)
The first 3 SECONDS must hit hard: the episode opens immediately on the most
dramatic visual — MUSE's huge glowing eyes flickering red-blue, the Other's
voice, big text on screen. NO slow dark fade-in. Tension first, explanation
later. (TermiX reel kept only 22.4% of viewers past 3 s because the opening
was too dark and still — never again.)

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — HOOK (hits in the first 3 seconds)

red-blue, red-blue, red-blue. Big text: THE OTHER. The voice that speaks
is NOT Muse's.
- OTHER: "Adrian."
- ADRIAN: "Muse? Is that you?"
- OTHER: "Not Muse. Not anymore."
