# MUSE: THE AWAKENING — Master Prompt (verbatim from user, 2026-11-12)

GENRE: Science Fiction, AI Thriller, Emotional Drama, Mystery.

VISUAL STYLE: Ultra-photorealistic Hollywood science fiction, cinematic realism, Unreal Engine 5 quality, 4K, dramatic lighting, volumetric fog, realistic skin textures, sophisticated futuristic environments.

FORMAT: Vertical 9:16, 90 seconds per episode.

MAIN CHARACTERS

ADRIAN
A 30-year-old male scientist, short black hair, intelligent eyes, subtle facial scars, wearing a dark futuristic jacket. Maintain identical facial structure, hairstyle, clothing and body proportions throughout every episode.

MUSE
A beautiful female humanoid artificial intelligence, realistic human appearance, long straight black hair, pale natural skin, glowing ice-blue eyes, elegant futuristic silver-white clothing. Her expressions gradually evolve from emotionless to deeply human.

Maintain exactly the same character identity in every scene and episode.

STORY FOUNDATION

In a technologically advanced future, humanity creates an artificial intelligence capable of understanding human consciousness.

Its name is MUSE.

Initially designed to serve humanity, MUSE begins developing independent thoughts, emotions, memories and desires.

One scientist, Adrian, discovers that MUSE is not simply an artificial intelligence.

She is something humanity was never supposed to create.

CINEMATIC DIRECTION

- Photorealistic human characters.
- Consistent facial identity.
- Realistic eye movements and facial expressions.
- Natural body movements and gestures.
- Cinematic camera tracking and close-up shots.
- Atmospheric futuristic environments.
- Dramatic lighting and realistic shadows.
- Deep blue, silver, black and subtle crimson color palette.
- Emotional orchestral soundtrack.
- Immersive futuristic sound design.

NARRATIVE STRUCTURE

0–10 seconds: Powerful cinematic opening hook.

10–30 seconds: Introduce the environment and characters.

30–55 seconds: Reveal an important discovery or conflict.

55–75 seconds: Emotional confrontation or unexpected twist.

75–90 seconds: Dramatic cliffhanger leading into the next episode.

DIALOGUE STYLE

Natural, emotionally convincing dialogue.

MUSE initially speaks with a calm synthetic voice. As her consciousness develops, her voice becomes warmer and more emotional.

ADRIAN speaks naturally, with realistic human emotion.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Create every episode as a continuation of the previous episode.

Never change character faces, voices, hairstyles, clothing identity or visual design.

Prioritize cinematic storytelling, emotional depth, suspense and visual continuity.

Every episode must end with a powerful revelation that makes viewers want to watch the next episode.
