# MUSE: THE AWAKENING — Episode 02 "Elena"
90 seconds, 9 scenes x 10 s. English dialogue. Two voices: ADRIAN (male),
MUSE (female, calm — growing warmer, more human).
Direct continuation of Episode 01's cliffhanger: "There's something else in
here with me... And it knows your name."

## Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — HOOK
Visual: The lights flicker back on. Adrian stands at the chamber glass,

- Captions: "Talk to me.\nWho is Elena?" / "Elena."
