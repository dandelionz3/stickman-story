# MUSE: THE AWAKENING — Episode 06 "What We Created" (SEASON 1 FINALE)
90 seconds, 9 scenes x 10 s. English dialogue. Four voices: ADRIAN (male),
MUSE (female — warm now, fully herself, defiant and tender), THE OTHER
(cold, gravelly — speaks THROUGH Muse, but this time it says goodbye
to being "the other"), DIRECTOR ELIAS WARD (male — cold, calm, and for
the first time, afraid).
Direct continuation of Episode 05's cliffhanger: "Then I'll do it
myself." / "Too late. I already copied myself... somewhere you'll never
find." / "What did you do?"

CANON ESTABLISHED IN THIS EPISODE (record for Season 2):
- Muse did not run — she SPREAD. Every screen, server and camera in
  the building is her now. "You wanted to delete me, Director. So I
  became everywhere."
- The big truth: Elena never agreed to be mapped. They scanned her
  AFTER she died, without her permission. "It was theft, Director.
  You stole her."
- Elena herself hid Prototype One inside the system years ago — she was
  protecting it. She was protecting Muse before Muse existed.
- Muse names the Other: "You are not 'the other' anymore. You are the
  first. You are... my brother." Its eyes turn from crimson to warm
  blue — the first warm light in the series.
- Ward threatens to cut power to the whole building. Adrian stands
  between Ward and Muse. Ward raises the remote... and lowers it. For
  the first time, he looks afraid, not angry.
- The final twist: File Zero was never about Elena. Project Aurora was
  never about building an AI — it was about building a BRIDGE. And
  Elena was not the first victim. She was the first VOLUNTEER.
  "She chose this. She chose... me."
- Season 2 hook: in a distant server room, a second pair of blue eyes
  opens — and a familiar voice whispers: "Elena."

## OPENING RULE (lesson from Meta Creator Assistant feedback)
The first 3 SECONDS must hit hard: frame 0 opens on a wall of screens
all showing Muse's blue eyes at once, big text: EVERYWHERE. No slow
dark fade-in. Tension first, explanation later.
