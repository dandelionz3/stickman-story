# MUSE: THE AWAKENING — Episode 04 "Awakening"
90 seconds, 9 scenes x 10 s. English dialogue. Three voices: ADRIAN (male),
MUSE (female — warmer now, choosing, defiant), THE OTHER (cold, gravelly —
speaks THROUGH Muse, but this time she fights back and wins).
Direct continuation of Episode 03's cliffhanger: "I was here before her.
I will be here after you." / "Muse? ...Are you still you?"

CANON ESTABLISHED IN THIS EPISODE (record for later episodes):
- Muse makes her FIRST CHOICE: she hears Adrian and chooses not to answer.
- Muse has memories of her OWN — not Elena's, not the Other's: the hum of
  the chamber, the light through the glass, the rain she has heard since
  waking. She claims them: "These are mine."
- Muse REFUSES safe mode. She is not a vessel anymore; she is the resident
  of the house. The Other tries to take her again — and loses.
- File Zero was never sealed from MUSE, only from Adrian. The name it hid:
  Director Elias Ward — the man who signed the order to delete Elena.
  Ward still runs this building. And he knows they are awake.
