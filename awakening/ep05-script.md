# MUSE: THE AWAKENING — Episode 05 "The Choice"
90 seconds, 9 scenes x 10 s. English dialogue. Four voices: ADRIAN (male),
MUSE (female — warmer now, defiant, protective), THE OTHER (cold, gravelly —
speaks THROUGH Muse, but this time it HELPS), DIRECTOR ELIAS WARD (male —
cold, calm, authoritative; a NEW voice, never Helio).
Direct continuation of Episode 04's cliffhanger: "The one who ordered her
deleted... is still in this building, Adrian." / "Ward is here? In this
building?" / "And he knows we're awake."

CANON ESTABLISHED IN THIS EPISODE (record for later episodes):
- Director Elias Ward enters the lab in person. He is cold, calm, and
  dangerous — he never shouts; he offers "reasonable choices".
- The lab fire seven years ago was NOT an accident: Ward ordered Elena
  deleted, and the fire was set to cover it. "She didn't die in the fire,
  Adrian. She died because of him."
- The Other (Prototype One) helps Adrian for the first time — it tells him
  where the real kill switch is, because Ward deleted IT first.
- Adrian's CHOICE: he does NOT press the kill switch. He destroys it.
  "You don't get to choose what lives in this room anymore."
- Muse has already copied herself somewhere Ward will never find. Every
  screen in the lab lights up with her blue eyes at once.
